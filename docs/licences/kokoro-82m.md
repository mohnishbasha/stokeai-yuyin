## Phonemiser (misaki)
- English (lang_code a/b): dictionary lookup from misaki/data/us_gold.json, us_silver.json, gb_gold.json, gb_silver.json, with spaCy tagging. espeak-ng is an optional OOD fallback (`espeak.EspeakFallback`); without it, "OOD words will be skipped" (kokoro pipeline.py warning). Licence of the dictionary data is not stated in the repo README (not verified; repo as a whole is Apache-2.0, provenance of the gold/silver lists not verified).
- Hindi (lang_code h): VOICES.md says "espeak-ng `hi`". KPipeline maps `h` to `hi` and builds `misaki.espeak.EspeakG2P`, which uses `phonemizer.backend.EspeakBackend` (phonemizer-fork, GPL-3.0 on PyPI) and `espeakng_loader` to supply libespeak-ng. So Hindi has no non-GPL path in the reference stack. Pipeline logs "Chunking logic not yet implemented" for EspeakG2P.
- misaki `[en]` extra pulls phonemizer-fork and espeakng-loader, and kokoro depends on `misaki[en]`, so stock `pip install kokoro` installs the GPL pieces even for English.

## Streaming
- Architecture: StyleTTS 2 with ISTFTNet decoder (card), non-autoregressive. No incremental output inside one forward pass was found. Streaming is at segment level: KPipeline is a generator yielding (graphemes, phonemes, audio) per split chunk (default split on newlines); kokoro-onnx `create_stream` yields per batch. Per-segment chunking only, not within a segment. Max phoneme length per call is bounded (MAX_PHONEME_LENGTH constant in kokoro-onnx).

## Commercial hosted use and container redistribution
Weights, kokoro, misaki and kokoro-onnx are permissive and allow hosted commercial use and bundling in a container. Risk 1 (training data): the card admits synthetic audio from closed commercial TTS providers; those providers' terms may restrict using output to train competing models, and no provider or contract is named, so this is an unquantified upstream legal risk that the Apache grant does not cure (no legal opinion obtained). Risk 2 (GPL): Hindi, and English OOD words, need espeak-ng via phonemizer(-fork) and espeakng-loader, which are GPL-3.0 and would taint a distributed container unless isolated or replaced (see espeak-ng.md). Risk 3: Hindi voices are grade C with minutes of data, and no Indian English voice exists. Risk 4: attribution duties for CC BY data are not triggered by Hindi or US English voices but are not verified per voice.
