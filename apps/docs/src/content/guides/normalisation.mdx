# Normalisation

Written text is turned into words before it is spoken. The same written form is read in the language of the sentence around it, so a Hindi sentence gets Hindi readings and an English sentence gets English readings. The table below is an illustration of the shape of this guide, not the full specification.

## Numbers

| Written | Hindi reading | English reading |
|---|---|---|
| 1,250 | एक हज़ार दो सौ पचास | one thousand two hundred fifty |
| 7 | सात | seven |

## Dates

| Written | Hindi reading | English reading |
|---|---|---|
| 02/01/2026 | दो जनवरी दो हज़ार छब्बीस | the second of January, two thousand twenty-six |

## Phone numbers

Digit strings such as one-time passwords and phone numbers are read one digit at a time.

| Written | Hindi reading | English reading |
|---|---|---|
| 482916 | चार आठ दो नौ एक छह | four eight two nine one six |

## Acronyms

Known acronyms are spelled out letter by letter. An organisation can add its own entries through the lexicon.

> Where this guide and the behaviour of the service differ, the service is wrong and the difference is a bug. Report it with the written text and the reading you expected.
